# C9 Funding Guide — demo narration

## Scene 1 — `01-home` · The site
**Say:** "Hi, I'm Kannishk. I'm the VP of Finance for College Nine Senate at UC Santa Cruz. Our funding process used to live in scattered docs and email threads, so I built this site to fix that. It runs on Drupal 11, and I built it myself, end to end."

## Scene 2 — `02-admin-edit` · Editing in Drupal
**Say:** "Here's the editing side. Every page is content in Drupal, so updating text or swapping a photo is a form, not a code change. I set it up so anyone on our board could learn to post an update in about ten minutes. That's the whole point of a CMS."

## Scene 3 — `03-transparency` · The budget, open
**Say:** "This is my favorite page. Every dollar students give us is visible here. The big number is what's left to request this year, and the table shows every decision we've made, including the ones we turned down and why. The page recalculates itself whenever an award changes."

## Scene 4 — `04-form` · The request form
**Say:** "Clubs used to dig through email to figure out how to ask for funding. Now it's one form, with the rules linked right at the top so nobody gets surprised later."

## Scene 5 — `05-form-filled` · Smart rules
**Say:** "The form knows our policies. This club is asking for three hundred fifty dollars, which is over the limit for outside organizations, so a justification box appears automatically. That used to cost clubs a two week resubmission. Now it's handled before they hit submit."

## Scene 6 — `06-confirmation` · A human answer
**Say:** "After submitting, students see exactly what happens next, with the real meeting time and the actual room number. It's a small thing, but people read it, and it saves our inbox a lot of nervous follow-up emails."

## Scene 7 — `07-guidelines` · Written for students
**Say:** "I wrote and proofread every page myself, and I cropped and prepared every photo from our real events. I'd love to bring this kind of care to the Admissions site. Thanks for watching."
