# Funding Transparency

Your student fees fund this budget, so you deserve to see where every dollar goes. This page shows the numbers, updated as the Senate allocates funds.

*Figures shown are sample data pending Senate board approval.*

## What you'll find here

- **This year's budget** — the total the Senate has to work with
- **Total spent** — what's been awarded so far
- **Remaining funds** — what's still available to request
- **Awards by organization** — every funded group and the amount they received

## Why we publish this

Open books keep the process fair. When you can see what's been funded and what's left, you can time your request better and set a realistic budget — and you can hold us accountable.

Spot something that looks off, or have a question about an award? Email [c9senate@ucsc.edu](mailto:c9senate@ucsc.edu).
