# College Nine Senate Funding

Got an event, a project, or a program that could use some money? That's what we're here for. The College Nine Senate funds student ideas — yours included.

**Who can apply:** any UCSC student organization, whether or not you're based at College Nine. Outside organizations can request up to **$200** (more is possible with written justification), and every funded event needs at least one College Nine affiliate able to take part.

## How it works

1. **Submit a request** using the form on this site.
2. **We reach out** within 1–2 days to schedule your presentation.
3. **You present** for 3–5 minutes at a weekly Senate meeting.
4. **You get a decision** by email, typically within 2–3 weeks.

That's the whole process. No hidden steps, no paperwork maze.

## Before you apply

- Read the [funding guidelines](funding-guidelines.md) — a few minutes now saves you a resubmission later.
- Check the [step-by-step guide](how-to-request.md) so you know what to prepare.
- Curious where the money goes? See our [transparency page](transparency-intro.md).

## When we meet

Senate meetings happen every **Thursday, 6:00–7:30pm** in **Social Sciences 1, Room 261**. Everyone is welcome to attend.

## Questions?

Email us at [c9senate@ucsc.edu](mailto:c9senate@ucsc.edu). We're students too — we'll give you a straight answer.
