# Funding Guidelines

Read this before you submit. It's short, and it covers everything that can hold a request up.

## What we can't fund

- **Food costs are not funded.** No exceptions — plan your budget without them.
- **Costume expenses are typically not funded.**
- **Travel costs are typically not considered** unless at least 25% of attendees are College Nine affiliates.

## What every event needs

All funded events must allow **at least one College Nine affiliate** to participate or be present. If your event is closed to College Nine students, we can't fund it.

## Request limits

Per the Constitution, organizations **outside College Nine** may request up to **$200**.

Want to request more than that? You'll need to include detailed written justification with your request. Without it, the request is returned to you for revision — which means a delay, so include it the first time.

## Tips for a smooth review

- Itemize your budget. Lump sums raise questions; line items answer them.
- Show the College Nine connection clearly — who from C9 benefits, attends, or participates.
- Submit early. Requests are reviewed in the order received, and decisions typically take 2–3 weeks.

Not sure whether your expense qualifies? Ask before you submit: [c9senate@ucsc.edu](mailto:c9senate@ucsc.edu).
