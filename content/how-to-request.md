# How to Request Funding

Requesting funding takes one form and one short presentation. Here's the full path from idea to decision.

## Step 1: Check the guidelines

Before anything else, skim the [funding guidelines](funding-guidelines.md). A few things — like food costs — can't be funded, and knowing that up front saves everyone time.

## Step 2: Submit the form

Fill out the funding request form on this site. Requests are reviewed in the order they come in, so submit as early as you can — especially if your event has a fixed date. Since decisions take 2–3 weeks, aim to submit **at least 4 weeks before your event**.

**Requesting more than $200 as an outside organization? That's allowed** — just include a detailed written justification for the amount with your request. Without one, the request is returned for revision, which costs you a week or two.

## Step 3: We contact you

Within **1–2 days**, the Senate will email you to schedule your presentation at an upcoming meeting.

## Step 4: Present at a Senate meeting

Prepare a **3–5 minute visual presentation** about your request. You'll give it at a weekly Senate meeting:

- **When:** Thursdays, 6:00–7:30pm
- **Where:** Social Sciences 1, Room 261

Keep it simple. Tell us what you're planning, who it's for, what it costs, and how it connects to College Nine.

### What makes a strong presentation

- A clear, itemized budget — where each dollar goes
- Who benefits, and how College Nine affiliates are included
- Realistic numbers for attendance and costs
- Slides or visuals that support your points instead of repeating them

## Step 5: Get your decision

Decisions typically arrive within **2–3 weeks** of your submission, by email.

Questions at any step? Email [c9senate@ucsc.edu](mailto:c9senate@ucsc.edu).
